# Transport Agreement — Sagar Roadlines

**Agreement ref:** SRL/BFC/2025-08
**Between:** BlueFin Commerce ("the Customer") and Sagar Roadlines ("the Carrier")
**Service:** regional surface transport, West Bengal and adjoining districts
**Term:** 1 August 2025 – 31 July 2027

## Freight charges

1. Freight per consignment is the sum of:
   - a **weight component of ₹6.00 per kg** of billed weight, and
   - a **distance component of ₹2.00 per km** of the booked lane distance.
2. Only standard service is offered under this agreement.

## Cold chain

3. Consignments booked as **cold chain** travel in refrigerated vehicles
   and carry a **premium of 20% on the freight charge** (the sum of the
   weight and distance components).

## Invoicing and payment

4. The Carrier invoices as trips are pooled, typically fortnightly. Payment
   is due 21 days from invoice date.
5. The consignment note (c-note) number quoted on each invoice line is the
   Carrier's reference and must correspond to a Customer booking. Lines
   that cannot be matched to a booking are not payable until reconciled.
6. The Carrier may issue **credit notes** correcting earlier invoices. A
   credit note references the original c-note number and the invoice it
   corrects, and must cover the full corrected amount.
