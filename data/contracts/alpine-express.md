# Services Agreement — Alpine Express Logistics

**Agreement ref:** AEL/BFC/2025-03
**Between:** BlueFin Commerce ("the Customer") and Alpine Express Logistics ("the Carrier")
**Service:** surface parcel and light cargo, South India network
**Term:** 1 March 2025 – 28 February 2027

## Freight charges

1. Freight is charged on **chargeable weight**, which is the higher of the
   consignment's actual weight and the **minimum chargeable weight of
   25 kg**.
2. Rates:
   - consignments **under 50 kg**: ₹9.50 per kg of chargeable weight
   - consignments **over 50 kg**: ₹8.25 per kg of chargeable weight
3. All bookings travel on standard service. Express products are not
   offered under this agreement.

## Handling

4. Consignments booked as **fragile** are handled under the Carrier's
   protected-handling process at **₹150.00 per consignment**, in addition
   to freight.

## Volume incentive

5. In any calendar month in which the Customer tenders **more than 12
   consignments**, the Carrier will apply a **5% discount to the invoice
   total** for that month's invoice.

## Invoicing and payment

6. The Carrier invoices monthly. Payment is due 30 days from invoice date.
7. Rate revisions require 60 days' written notice and take effect from the
   first day of the following calendar month.
